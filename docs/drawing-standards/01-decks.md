# 01 — Decks (Exterior, Open)

Reference: **AWC DCA 6-2015** (Prescriptive Residential Wood Deck Construction Guide) and **IRC 2021 Sections R507, R311**.

Applies to single-level decks attached to or freestanding from one- and two-family dwellings, max 40 psf live + 10 psf dead load, joists/beams of southern pine, Douglas fir-larch, hem-fir, spruce-pine-fir, or redwood No. 2 or better.

| Stair detail | 1-1/2" = 1'-0" | Stringer-to-deck, riser/tread dimensions, handrail profile, lighting note |

## Post Sizing (DCA 6 Table 7)

| Tributary area | ≤ 8' height | 9'–14' height |
|---------------|-------------|---------------|
| ≤ 48 sf | 4×4 | 6×6 |
| 49–72 sf | 6×6 | 6×6 |
| 73–168 sf | 6×6 | 6×6 |

All deck posts must be **pressure-treated** (UC4A ground contact minimum, UC4B if direct burial).

## Footings (IRC R403 + DCA 6 Fig. 9)

Minimum bearing area at 1,500 psf soil:

| Tributary load area | Minimum footing diameter | Minimum thickness |
|--------------------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| ≤ 20 sf | 12" round | 8" |
| 21–40 sf | 16" round | 8" |
| 41–60 sf | 20" round | 8" |
| 61–80 sf | 23" round | 8" |
| 81–100 sf | 26" round | 10" |

Bottom of footing must extend below local **frost depth**. Use post base connector with stand-off (e.g., Simpson ABU, PB) — no direct embed in concrete unless using preservative-treated to UC4B and the AHJ accepts it.

## Ledger Connection to House (IRC R507.9)

Use **1/2" lag screws or through-bolts** (not nails) into 2× band joist or LVL rim of at least 1" thickness.

| Joist span | Lag/bolt spacing |
|-----------|------------------|
| ≤ 6' | 30" |
| 6'–8' | 23" |
| 8'–10' | 18" |
| 10'–12' | 15" |
| 12'–14' | 13" |
| 14'–16' | 11" |
| 16'–18' | 10" |

**Rules:**
- Edge distance: top/bottom 2" min, end 2" min, between rows ≥ 1-5/8".
- Stagger in two rows.
- Continuous flashing over ledger; do not rely on caulk alone (IRC R703.4).
- **Lateral load connection required** — 4 hold-down devices (Simpson DTT2Z or equivalent) tying joists to floor framing inside house, OR full DCA 6 lateral connection per Fig. 16/17 (IRC R507.9.2).
- **Never attach a ledger to a brick veneer, cantilevered floor, or untreated rim where rot is suspected** — convert to freestanding deck.

## Guards (IRC R312)

- Required when walking surface > 30" above grade (measured within 36" horizontally).
- Min height **36"** at single-family residential.
- Openings shall not allow passage of a **4" sphere** (4-3/8" for guards alongside stair treads).
- Top rail must resist 200 lb concentrated load in any direction.
- Guard post anchorage: tension hold-down at base — use Simpson DTT2Z, HD7B, or blocked + bolted assembly. Toenailed 4×4 posts **do not** meet the 200 lb load requirement.

## Stairs (IRC R311.7)

| Item | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Riser height (max) | 7-3/4" |
| Tread depth (min) | 10" |
| Variation in risers/treads in same flight | 3/8" |
| Min headroom | 6'-8" |
| Min width | 36" above handrail |
| Handrail height | 34"–38" |
| Handrail required when | 4 or more risers |

- Stringers: 2×12 minimum, max span 13'-3" with cut stringers, or 18'-0" with solid stringers (DCA 6 Table 8).
- Treads: (2) 2×6 or (1) 5/4 deck board + nosing.
- Stair guards required when total rise > 30"; same height & opening rules as deck guards.

## Decking

| Material | Min thickness (joists @ 16" o.c.) | Min thickness (24" o.c.) |
|----------|----------------------------------|-------------------------|
| 2× lumber (1-1/2" actual) | 1-1/2" | 1-1/2" |
| 5/4 radius-edge | 1" | Not permitted |
| Composite | Per mfr. | Per mfr. (often 16" o.c. max) |

**Fastening:** 2 screws (10d nail equivalent or #8 deck screw) per board per joist. Self-spacing for water drainage (~1/8").

## General Notes Block (paste into drawings)

```
1. Construction per IRC 2021 and AWC DCA 6-2015.
2. Design loads: 40 psf live + 10 psf dead. Snow/wind/seismic per location.
3. Lumber: No. 2 or better, [species], pressure-treated to UC4A min where
   in contact with weather, UC4B for direct ground contact.
4. Fasteners: Hot-dipped galvanized ASTM A153 or stainless steel where in
   contact with PT lumber. ACQ-compatible.
5. Joists @ [16/24]" o.c. with double rim joist and joist hangers (Simpson
   LUS or equal). Toenailing not permitted in lieu of hangers.
6. Ledger flashing: corrosion-resistant metal, lapped to WRB above; gap
   ledger from siding 1/2" for drainage.
7. Posts on Simpson post bases above grade; no wood-to-concrete contact.
8. Guard posts: hold-down hardware at all locations; verify 200 lb load
   per IRC R301.5.
9. Stair handrails graspable per IRC R311.7.8.5.
10. Contractor to verify footing soil bearing; report unsuitable conditions
    to designer before placing concrete.
```

## Items Outside Prescriptive Scope (require engineer)

- Multi-level decks with shared posts.
- Deck heights > 14' to walking surface.
- Cantilevers > 1/4 of backspan or > 4'.
- Hot tub or planter loads beyond 60 psf.
- Decks supporting roofs (then it's a porch — see `02-porches.md`).
- Engineered lumber as joists or beams (provide manufacturer span tables).
